Implemented checks
- Spleef standard Snowball unlock, per-player cooldown, one-block floor impact and automatic replenishment are server-authoritative.
- Spleef Power Egg delayed random awards, random intervals, finite stacking cap and horizontal five-block impact are server-authoritative.
- CTF/Domination boost configuration supports manual/automatic placement, active cap, initial delay, random respawn range, minimum spacing, allowed boost types, per-type durations/colors and armor points.
- Manual boost positions are registered through the Minigame Setup Tool and validated for uniqueness, region containment, clear space and sturdy ground.
- Automatic CTF candidates are distributed through the arena; automatic Domination candidates are generated near linked node respawns.
- Floating boost item entities use no gravity and no entity glow; colored dust particles provide the surrounding mist.
- Pickup is proximity-controlled, consumes the active boost and schedules a replacement after a random configured interval.
- Match finish removes active boost entities and restores temporary armor. Startup removes orphan boost entities.
- Recovery schema 3 stores the original base armor attribute so logout/crash recovery cannot leave temporary armor behind.
- Spleef, CTF and Domination editors expose all new settings.
