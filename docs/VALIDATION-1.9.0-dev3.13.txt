Dedicated Jail design:
- Physical Jails are a separate subsystem and must be fully inside a parent Region.
- Mines remain independent and may be nested inside a Jail.
- Choice / Task-only / Time-only punishment paths are explicit.
- Choice timeout is 30 seconds; insufficient buyout funds locks Task.
- Task deadlines use absolute timestamps; failure permanently bans with exact reason: failed to complete punishment.
- Jail restrictions remove operator/permission bypass while jailed and redirect shared SSU dashboard entry points to the Jail dashboard.
- Active prisoners are confined to their Jail/work/cell rules; admins may teleport to prisoners, but prisoners cannot remain teleported outside Jail.
- Jail supersedes Freeze cleanly so freeze anchoring cannot fight Jail confinement.
- Offline admin release preserves a pending pre-Jail state restore instead of discarding the backup.
- Expired time-only sentences restore/release immediately on reconnect instead of re-entering the cell first.
