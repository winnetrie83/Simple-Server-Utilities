Mines scope in dev3.11
----------------------
This build intentionally delivers Mines phase 1/foundation: persistence, GUI, setup tool,
permissions, weighted reset palette, bounded resets, interval/mined-threshold triggers,
warnings, safe player handling, teleport positions and progress/basic counters.
Advanced roadmap items such as true inventory-slot palette authoring, custom/no-drop rules,
XP/Fortune/Silk controls, integrated mine holograms and richer dedicated mine statistics
remain follow-up work and are not claimed as complete in this build.
