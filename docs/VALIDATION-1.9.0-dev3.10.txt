Implemented scope
-----------------
- Threaded persistent Support / Reports conversations.
- Fixed ticket categories: Help, Bug, Player report, Suggestion, Appeal, Other.
- Dedicated known-player picker for Player report tickets.
- Rich-text ticket creation and replies for players and staff using SSU's 16-colour + bold/italic/underline/strikethrough editor.
- Server-side rich-text sanitising and bounded ticket/message payloads.
- Player/staff unread state and online notifications.
- Staff assignment, resolve, reopen and close workflow.
- First staff reply auto-assigns an unassigned ticket.
- Player reply to a resolved ticket reopens it / returns it to Assigned when an assignee remains.
- Ticket-list paging, admin status filters and selected-thread paging.
- Schema-1 message/staffNote migration into schema-2 threaded history.
