Implemented checks
- Speed, Regeneration, Armor and Jump boost visuals resolve to Golden Boots, Golden Apple, Diamond Chestplate and Rabbit Foot.
- Boost pickup sounds resolve to firework launch, beacon power-select, diamond armor equip and wind-charge throw.
- Boost pickup sound packets are spatial and restricted to active players in the same match and dimension.
- The infinite Spleef Snowball uses the native item cooldown manager and reapplies the remaining cooldown on an early click.
- A valid infinite Snowball throw temporarily provides the vanilla use path with a second copy; normal use consumes that copy and the match tick enforces an exact maximum of one remaining visible projectile.
- Denied early clicks return a successful cancelled interaction and explicitly resynchronize the inventory to prevent a client-side ghost-empty slot.
- Snowball and Power Egg entities are normalized on entity join to the exact player look vector, with no gravity and no random spread.
- Manual CTF and Domination boost locations receive temporary End Rod setup markers.
- Moving a configured boost index removes its previous End Rod before the new marker is restored.
- Match startup and snapshot capture call the shared physical setup-marker cleanup, which now removes boost End Rods as well as temporary banners.
