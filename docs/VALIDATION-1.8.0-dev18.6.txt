Safety observations validated statically
- A claim peak is raised on every successful chunk addition and repaired to at least the current live size during load/access.
- Removing chunks never lowers the current cycle peak.
- Tax rate, interval, reminder lead and dimension multiplier are snapshotted per claim cycle.
- Reminder text labels the amount as an estimate and explicitly documents expansion/shrink behavior and consequences.
- Player deletion routes cannot remove the final taxable claim area without the GUI settlement payload.
- Settlement totals are validated against immutable per-claim recovery snapshots before the ledger accepts them.
- Economy debits use a settlement UUID idempotency key; only explicit `insufficient_funds` may enter automatic forfeiture.
- Automatic non-payment snapshots all current claims/Homes for removal, while monetary amount and permanent penalty are derived from the claims actually due in that settlement.
- Dimension multipliers affect tax money only; permanent collateral uses unweighted peak chunks.
- Claims, Homes and permanent limit records are flushed and exact-path verified before journal progress advances.
- Permanent capacity penalties are keyed by settlement UUID and checked for conflicting amounts before completion.
- Completed penalty-bearing journals are retained and reconciled with limit records on startup.
- Corrupt, ambiguous or non-durable recovery state enters a restart-persistent safety halt through `tax_safety_halt.json`.
- Claim mutation and Home creation/movement are locked during an active owner settlement; unrelated players remain unaffected.
