Implemented correction
- The World Map marker context overlay is treated as modal for left clicks.
- Context-menu hit detection runs in WorldMapScreen before child-widget dispatch and therefore before the underlying map widget can receive or focus the event.
- Add marker uses the full 126 x 20 first-row hitbox for empty terrain.
- Existing markers use separate 60 x 20 Edit and 62 x 20 Delete/Confirm hitboxes.
- Close uses the 126 x 20 second-row hitbox.
- A click outside the menu closes it and is consumed to prevent click-through.
- The rendered context buttons remain present for visual state, narration and tooltips; their actions are mirrored by the modal screen-level hit handling.
