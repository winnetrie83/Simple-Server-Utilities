Implemented changes
- The former horizontal ground ring is replaced by a camera-facing circular billboard basis derived from the current camera-to-marker vector.
- The marker icon is centred at the saved marker block centre while the beam remains an independent full-height vertical line.
- The marker editor exposes all sixteen Minecraft legacy colours in two rows of eight.
- Colour controls render full colour swatches with selection/hover outlines and named plus hexadecimal tooltips.
- The World Map marker context actions are enclosed in a larger padded double-outline frame that remains clamped inside the map viewport.
