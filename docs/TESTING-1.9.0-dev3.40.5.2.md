# SSU 1.9.0-dev3.40.5.2 test focus

This is an isolation build for Player-NPC held items.

1. Start Minecraft and confirm the title screen renders normally.
2. Load a world with an existing Player NPC and confirm its skin/model still renders.
3. Give the NPC a mainhand item and verify the item appears on the correct arm.
4. Give the NPC an offhand item and verify the second item appears.
5. Test Wide/Steve and Slim/Alex skins.
6. Test sword/tool, shield, bow and crossbow while idle and during combat.
7. Confirm NPC AI/pathfinding remains as in dev3.40.4.1.
8. Confirm NPC Tool -> right click air opens only one NPC Manager UI.

Armor is intentionally not enabled in this build. Do not use armor visibility as a pass/fail criterion yet.
